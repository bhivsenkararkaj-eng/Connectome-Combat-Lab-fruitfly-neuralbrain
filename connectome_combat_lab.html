Neurotransmitter (top_nt)

What's real here: every point in the 3D nebula behind the arena is an actual neuron's soma position from the published male Drosophila whole-brain connectome, colour-coded by its real class. Cell type names in the Circuit tab are real, dataset-verified identities.

What's simulated: this file contains neuron annotations, not synapse weights — so the connection strengths that drive behaviour are evolved by the genetic algorithm, not measured from real synapses. Depth (z) in the nebula is visually stretched ~4× since the real dataset is comparatively flat on that axis.
